From Pastry Artist to Coding Instructor

Hi! I’m Teacher Roma, and my journey started with my love for creativity, food, and hands-on learning.

I graduated with a degree in Hotel and Restaurant Management from Centro Escolar University (CEU) Manila in 2013. I also studied Professional Baking and Pastry Arts at Global Academy in Timog, Quezon City.

My pastry life started in Brunei Darussalam, where I worked as a Pastry Decorator and Pastry Artist at Fleur de Lys Brunei.

When the COVID-19 pandemic began in 2020, I had to return to the Philippines. During that time, I started offering online pastry goods under the name Little Mermaid Caloocan.

The pandemic also opened a new path for me. I started taking online training from India, where I learned coding, programming, and how to teach technology to children.

My first coding teaching experience began with WhiteHat Jr., a technology education company founded in Mumbai, India. WhiteHat Jr. later became part of BYJU’S FutureSchool.

As my experience grew, I joined OneSpace Education, a STEM and technology education company based in Hanoi, Vietnam. Through OneSpace, I continued teaching coding to students from different backgrounds using fun and project-based lessons.

I also taught at Algorithmics San Juan, located in San Juan City, Metro Manila, Philippines. This helped me grow as a face-to-face classroom instructor.

I became part of Geeklama, an online coding and STEM school based in the United States. Geeklama provides live online classes for children in coding, game development, web development, and AI.

Today, I am proudly connected with iCode Junior, an international STEM education company based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

Here in the Philippines, I also conduct face-to-face classes through Tinkers Corner, a learning center based in Metro Manila, Philippines. I teach coding, multimedia arts, and also offer kids’ baking and pastry workshops.

Today, I am grateful to combine my two passions: technology and baking. My goal is to help every child learn with confidence, creativity, and joy.

My Mission

My mission is to provide a fun, supportive, and creative learning experience where children can explore technology, arts, and baking while building confidence and problem-solving skills.
