My Story

Hi! I'm Teacher Roma, and I believe that learning should always be exciting, creative, and meaningful.

My journey started when I graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Hotel and Restaurant Management from Centro Escolar University (CEU) Manila in 2013. Wanting to specialize further, I also completed Professional Baking and Pastry Arts at Global Academy in Timog, Quezon City.

My professional pastry career began in the beautiful country of Brunei Darussalam, where I worked as a Pastry Decorator and Pastry Artist at Fleur de Lys Brunei. During those years, I developed my skills in cake decorating, artisan pastries, desserts, and creative baking. Every cake taught me patience, precision, and the importance of paying attention to every little detail.

When the COVID-19 pandemic began in 2020, like millions of people around the world, I had to return home to the Philippines. Although it was a difficult transition, it also became the beginning of another wonderful journey.

While staying at home, I started accepting online orders for cakes, pastries, cupcakes, cookies, and customized desserts. This small business eventually became known as Little Mermaid Caloocan, where I continued sharing my love for baking with families in the community.

During the pandemic, I realized that learning never stops. I enrolled in virtual programming and coding teacher training conducted from India. What started as curiosity quickly became another passion.

My first teaching experience began with WhiteHat Jr., one of the pioneers in live online coding education for children. When WhiteHat Jr. became part of BYJU'S FutureSchool, I continued teaching students from different countries, helping children develop computational thinking, creativity, and confidence through coding.

As my experience grew, I joined OneSpace, an online learning platform where I continued teaching coding and technology to children from different parts of the world. Through OneSpace, I handled students with different learning styles while creating personalized lessons that made coding enjoyable and easy to understand.

I was also given the opportunity to teach face-to-face classes at Algorithmics San Juan, an international coding school that focuses on teaching children programming, digital literacy, and logical thinking through project-based learning.

I later became part of Qlama, an online education platform that connects learners with experienced instructors through interactive virtual classes. This experience allowed me to continue helping students improve both their technical and creative skills.

Today, I am proudly teaching with iCode Junior, an international STEM education company offering coding, robotics, AI, game development, web development, app development, and other technology programs designed especially for children.

Here in the Philippines, I also conduct classes through Tinker's World, a creative learning center that encourages children to explore coding, robotics, 3D design, engineering, arts, and other hands-on educational experiences.

Today, I am fortunate to combine my two greatest passions: technology and baking.

Whether I'm helping a child build their first website, create a Roblox game, program in Python, design a mobile app, or decorate their very first cupcake, my mission remains the same:

To inspire children to become confident, creative, curious, and lifelong learners.
