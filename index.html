My Story

Hi, I’m Teacher Roma. My journey started with my love for creativity, food, and hands-on learning. I graduated with a degree in HRM from CEU Manila in 2013, and I also studied Professional Baking and Pastry Arts at Global Academy in Timog.

My pastry life started in Brunei, where I worked as a pastry decorator and pastry artist at Fleur de Lys Brunei. There, I learned how to create beautiful pastries, decorate cakes, and turn simple ingredients into something special and memorable.

When the COVID-19 pandemic started in 2020, I had to return to the Philippines. Like many others, I needed to adjust and find new ways to continue my passion. During that time, I started offering online pastry goods under the name Little Mermaid in Caloocan.

The pandemic also opened a new path for me. I joined online training from India through virtual classes, where I started learning coding, programming, and teaching technology to kids. This became the beginning of my journey as a coding instructor.

My first coding teaching experience started with WhiteHat Jr., which later became part of BYJU’S. After that, I continued teaching with OneSpace, where I gained more experience in online coding education for children. OneSpace helped me grow as an instructor and gave me the opportunity to teach students from different backgrounds.

I also became part of Algorithmics in San Juan, where I had the opportunity to teach face-to-face coding classes. This helped me improve not only as an online teacher, but also as a classroom instructor who can guide students personally and hands-on.

I have also worked with Qlama, an online learning platform that connects students with teachers for different educational programs and skill-based learning. Through this, I continued helping students develop confidence in learning technology.

Currently, I am also connected with iCode Junior, a coding and STEM education company that offers technology courses for kids, including coding, game development, robotics, web development, and other digital skills.

Here in the Philippines, I also started accepting classes through Tinker Corners or Tinker’s World, a creative learning space that supports kids in exploring hands-on activities, technology, creativity, and project-based learning.

Today, I combine both of my passions: coding and baking. As a coding tutor, I help kids learn programming, game development, web design, and creative technology. As a baking workshop instructor, I guide children in creating cupcakes, cookies, cakes, and fun pastry projects.

My goal is to help kids learn with confidence, creativity, and joy. Whether they are building their first game or decorating their first cupcake, I want every child to feel proud of what they can create.
